## Model aims and claims

The PP/NF framework proposes that an effective spatial-dimension transition softens gravity near dense cores and aims to avoid traditional central singularities. The supplied draft proposes modified weak-field metric perturbations and effective-potential expressions, and claims all-radius orbital stability and elimination of the standard ISCO.

Additional numerical claims in the supplied materials include a maximum mass of approximately \(9.27\,M_\odot\) and a high-frequency gravitational-wave scale near \(10^{18}\,\mathrm{Hz}\).

These values and implications are recorded as proposals or claims made by the model, not as measured quantities.

## Assessment targets

The model materials identify the following items as necessary for quantitative assessment:

1. Specify the metric, coordinates, field equations, and matching boundary conditions.
2. Derive geodesic motion and stability criteria from the same spacetime model.
3. Define an equation of state and solve the stellar interior equations with regularity and boundary conditions.
4. Calculate quantitative observable signatures for comparison with compact-star and gravitational-wave data.
